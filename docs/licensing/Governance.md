# Gru Governance

**Product:** Gru (GRU)  
**Publisher:** LedoCorp  
**Primary maintainer:** Shawn Londono  
**Model:** **BDFL** (Benevolent Dictator For Life) — maintainer-led  

Gru is maintained under an independent **BDFL** model. **Pull requests are not accepted** on the official public repository (see [Contributing.md](Contributing.md)).

## Core Principles

- **BDFL / Maintainer Authority**  
  Shawn Londono (LedoCorp) has final decision-making authority over the official repository, releases, license terms, and project direction.

- **No public PR merge program**  
  The tree is published for use and forking under GPLv3, not as a community merge target.

- **Forks welcome**  
  Anyone may fork under GPLv3 and build independently.

- **Selective adoption**  
  Useful external work may be referenced or adapted by the maintainer with attribution; that is not a PR merge program and does not require a CLA for day-to-day BDFL operation.

- **Independence**  
  The project is developed and maintained independently. A commercial dual-license (GPLv3 **or** a paid proprietary-use grant) may be offered later; it is deferred and separate from this GPLv3 cut.

- **Transparency**  
  Major public decisions may be noted in release notes or docs when relevant.
